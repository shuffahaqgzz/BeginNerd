# PRODUCT-FOUNDATION-02 — Product Requirements

**Trigger:** The user needs product requirements, acceptance criteria, a product requirements document, or to revise a product hypothesis after verified feedback.

## Procedure
1. Inspect the Product Brief, customer interview/experiment evidence if available, approved scope, existing requirements and known stakeholder feedback. Preserve valid brownfield content.
2. For each material need, record a stable product requirement identifier such as REQUIREMENTS-001, actor, user outcome, value, priority/criticality, evidence source and current approval.
3. Define observable acceptance criteria such as ACCEPTANCE-CRITERIA-001, including meaningful negative/error cases. Distinguish an acceptance condition from an executed test result.
4. Classify requirements derived from verified user evidence vs Owner assumption or untested market hypothesis. Do not treat a product idea as customer-proven.
5. Capture data sensitivity, access, retention, privacy, budget, hosting and reuse constraints proportionately; detailed security design and final technology selection belong to Phase 2.
6. Explain relevant tradeoffs, resolve conflicts, recommend priorities and obtain **one informed approval for a coherent group of material choices**, not a new approval for every sentence.
7. Produce the relevant section of PRODUCT.md or PRODUCT-REQUIREMENTS-DOC.md when file writes are authorized. Keep PROPOSED and APPROVED distinct.
8. Reconcile requirement IDs with acceptance, minimum viable product scope and technical constraints before handing off.

## Do not
Do not invent requirements, approval, customer quotes or completed experiments. Do not create formal coding tasks or treat a product requirements document as coding/deployment authorization.

## Completion
All critical product needs in scope are understandable, linked to relevant acceptance and evidence (or clearly labeled assumptions), and have known next decision owners. This does not alone establish Product Baseline Readiness.
