# SOLUTION-06 — Technology Selection / Research & Reuse

**Input/trigger:** Existing repo/tooling/lockfiles, architectural and license/cost constraints. Run when this specific solution domain is requested; respect current G1 scope and approval.

## Procedure
1. Inventory what works already; do not duplicate installs or scaffold before examining alternatives.
2. Research relevant software, frameworks, API/SDK, no-change and custom alternatives proportionate to impact.
3. For material choices record verified source + date, versions, license obligations, security applicability, support/EOL, compatibility and cost.
4. Recommend Adopt/Extend/Integrate/Custom based on best fit, obtain material Owner approval and record the decision.
5. Link each material decision to source REQUIREMENTS / ACCEPTANCE IDs, evidence and timestamp where available; mark missing facts TBD and assign a decision owner.

**Canonical output:** TECHNOLOGY-SELECTION.md or Lean decision. Lean/Structured is adaptive; do not require every design document.
**Acceptance for this design workflow:** Approved material choices documented with evidence; no package installation.
**Governance:** Explain → Interview → Approve → Record; write only within an authorized path. A planned verification is NOT EXECUTED. No formal task decomposition and no change of application runtime.
